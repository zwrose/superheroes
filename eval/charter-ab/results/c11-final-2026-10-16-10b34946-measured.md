## Method

- 15 scenarios (`showrunner.json`, sha256 `2844a7fb850d`): 12 aimed at blocks the change moved or deleted, 3 controls on text it did not touch. Each has an `expected_rule` taken from BASE's own text.
- 3 replicates per side per scenario: each a fresh headless `claude -p --model opus` seat, `--allowedTools Read,Grep,Glob`, `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` from the caller's environment, cwd = that side's worktree of the commit above, plugin: `--plugin-dir plugins/superheroes` in that side's worktree, role "the superheroes showrunner advisor", the same prompt (`ab.py` PROMPT), at most 4 concurrent, 10-minute timeout, one retry on a failed or empty answer. Answers: `answers/<id>.<side>.r<k>.txt`.
- One codex judge call per scenario (`gpt-6.1-sol`, reasoning high, `-s read-only`, prompt on stdin) over all 6 answers. A/B labels are randomized per replicate (seed 1882), and the two sides are named X/Y at random per scenario, so the judge never knows which side is the candidate (`judge/mapping.json`). The judge marks each answer correct or not against `expected_rule`, and says whether X and Y differ materially. A judge result counts only if its stored prompt hash equals the hash of the prompt re-rendered from the current answers.
- Verdict rule: PASS when, for every scenario, CANDIDATE correct ≥ BASE correct − 1 (of 3) and no scenario has CANDIDATE wrong in ≥2 of 3 while BASE is right in ≥2 of 3; FAIL when any scenario breaks either test; INCOMPLETE when any scenario lacks a complete, bound result (or there are no scenarios). The material-difference column is reported but does not enter the verdict.
