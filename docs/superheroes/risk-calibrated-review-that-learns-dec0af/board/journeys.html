How review works after the redesign

Four journeys, drawn from the framing and your rulings through 2026-10-09. Comment on anything that's wrong or missing; the open choices board comes next, then the build board with the actual sheets.

You An agent (builder or advisor) An independent reviewer

Journey 1 · once per project

Setting up the risk profile

The advisor opens setupOne sitting, together with "who it's for". If "who it's for" is already set, the sitting covers risk and trust and only offers a look at the existing answer.

↓

You answer in product termsWhere money, customer data, access and irreversible actions live; which outcomes are unacceptable; how fast a bad change can be undone; what can be run to test.

↓

Saved as one "Risk and trust" itemYour threat model, plus the risk areas the loop reads mechanically.

↓

Until it's set: unclear changes go to the full laneand every leftover with a consequence comes to you.

Journey 2 · every change

A change gets reviewed

Routing picks the laneTouches a risk area: full lane, whatever the size. A model may raise the lane, never lower it.

↓

The builder builds

↓

The panel reviewsFull lane: a generalist, the test role (break-it tests, false passes, flakiness, bloat), and the specialists your risk areas call for. Light lane: one reviewer. All from a different model family than every maker; all get the threat model.

↓

Each finding states its consequenceNo consequence beyond code quality: craft, decided and recorded. A real consequence: fixed, shown wrong, or left for you.

↓

Fix rounds until the obligations are metRe-review focuses on the fix. Rulings reach the next fix. Nothing found is lost. Capped rounds stop and disclose.

↓

The review record is writtenBy code: lane and why, who reviewed, what was found and what happened to it, CI on the final commit, what's left, cost.

↓

The advisor vets from the record

Journey 3 · when you sit down to decide

Your decision walk

Batch 1: what blocks workIn the chat and on the index sheet (visuals live on the index). Answer either place. Each item leaves the index once answered.

↓

The advisor executes what batch 1 unblocked

↓

Batch 2: other calls not tied to a PRIn the chat.

↓

The advisor executes batch 2, then builds the PR sheetsBuilt only now, because a ruling can send a PR back.

↓

Batch 3: one sheet per PR, plus the indexThe index lists PRs in merge order: needs you, being fixed, ready. Each sheet: the owner half, every acceptance answered on its own, then the merge verdict, which unlocks only when every acceptance is accepted. The index and the sheets are drawn on the build board (board 3) before the spec is written.

↓

You say "done" in the chat

↓

Merges in orderAnswers and verdict written back to each PR.
Send-backs and follow-upsFix-before-merge goes back to the builder; follow-ups are filed.

Journey 4 · over weeks

The loop learns from escapes

A bug is traced to a reviewed PRSeen at a vet of a fix, a detective diagnosis, a field report, or your own report. Later: the project's error tracker, where it has one.

↓

It's logged as a review escapeIn the misses log, linked to the PR's record: was it missed, or seen and left?

↓

The gardening pass reads the evidenceEscapes, plus each reviewer seat on the keep-or-retire list.

↓

You rule on a proposal"Two left-over billing issues became bugs: treat billing as a risk area?" Tightening and loosening both need your yes.

↓

Your ruling goes to Canonand the profile changes from the next review on.

Once, when an existing project adopts the release

Upgrading a project that already uses superheroes

Its threat model becomes the prose half of "Risk and trust", unchangedThe risk areas start empty, so until the sitting: unclear changes go to the full lane, and every leftover with a consequence comes to you.

↓

The release notes ask you to do the risk sitting with your advisorNot blocking: the project keeps working on the safe default above until you do.

↓

Reviews in flight at the upgrade finish on the old version or restart onceOld certification files stay on disk, unread. Review seat pins carry over; the test seat's pin becomes the merged test role's.

↓

Learning starts counting at adoptionThe misses log gains review escapes from that day; each review seat joins the keep-or-retire list with its window starting then.

Once, at rollout

What retires, in the same step as the review record lands

The certificate gate, the planted-bug review control, relocate and re-emit, resuming old saved sessions, and the exact-head evidence chain. Every rule that pointed at them points at the review record instead. Sessions in flight at the upgrade restart once.
