4 · In configure

"Risk and trust"

Risk and trust

What we defend against

Your threat model, as you wrote it. Unchanged by the upgrade.

Risk areas

  • Payments: charging, refunds and receipts. Hard to undo once a customer is charged.
  • Customer data: accounts, addresses and order history. A leak can't be undone.
  • Sign-in and access: who can see or change what.
  • Database changes: undoable only from a backup.

A change that touches a risk area gets the full lane, the specialists it calls for, and break-it tests on that area. Until risk areas are set, unclear changes get the full lane and every leftover with a consequence comes to you.

Set up in one sitting with the advisor

  1. Who the product is for, and what it's for. When that's already set, the advisor only offers to read it back.
  2. Where money, customer data, access and irreversible actions live.
  3. Which outcomes are unacceptable.
  4. How quickly a bad change can be undone.
  5. What can be run to try the product.
