**Register entries this child consumes (quoted verbatim from docs/superheroes/risk-calibrated-review-that-learns-dec0af/register.md; the register-quote check runs at filing, intake, and the package read):**

> **R1 — The review record.** When a review ends, on every lane (full, light and micro), the plugin writes exactly one review record for the PR, through one record writer in `plugins/superheroes/lib/` that every lane's end-of-review step calls and no other surface duplicates. The record holds: the lane and why the change got it; which reviewers were planned and which ran, each with its model family; every finding the reviewers raised, with its stated consequence, its outcome and the reason for that outcome; the final commit and the CI result on it; what is left for the owner; the number of rounds, and whether the loop stopped at its round cap; any planned review that did not run, with the owner's ruling or word that let the PR go ahead without it, if there is one; and the cost. Facts that code holds are taken from code, never from a session's account: the lane, the final commit, the check results, and, for each reviewer an engine ran, that it ran, read from the engine's own record. Where only the session that ran the review reports a reviewer's run, the record says "reported by the session" beside that reviewer. The record keeps every finding, craft decisions and findings shown wrong included, readable after the PR merges, and the PR names its record so the advisor's vet, the sheet builder and a misses-log entry each reach it in one hop. No record says that a change has no bugs. The record's file shape, its home for each PR, how the PR names it, and the unit the cost is stated in are decide-by: C1.

> **R2 — One release.** Every child of this epic merges to main before the plugin release that carries any of it, and that one release carries all of it: no child cuts a release or asks for one, the advisor holds the release pull request until the last child has merged, and the review record (C1) and the retirement of the five certificate parts (C2) never ship apart. Each child's consumer-visible change gets its `plugins/superheroes/TRANSITION.md` note in the one unreleased section that release will carry.

> **R3 — Risk and trust, and how the loop reads it.** Configure holds one item named "Risk and trust": the project's existing threat model (`threatModel` in `plugins/superheroes/lib/project_config.py`), unchanged, plus a list of risk areas, each naming which parts of the project it covers, why it is risky, and how hard a mistake there is to undo. The covered parts are stored in a form code can match against a change, and one reader in `plugins/superheroes/lib/` answers which risk areas a change touches; every consumer (the lane call, the panel, the test role) calls that reader and none re-derives the match, so a change that plainly sits inside an area is matched with no model judgment. When the item is missing, holds no risk areas, or cannot be read, the reader reports the risk areas as not set, and the plugin then gives every change whose risk is unclear the full lane and brings every leftover with a real consequence to the owner. Whether the item keeps the threat-model item's number and slug, the stored shape of a risk area, the matching form and the reader's interface are decide-by: C4; what makes a change's risk unclear when no risk areas are set is decide-by: C5, recorded for the owner's veto.

> **R5 — Independence: who may review.** Every reviewer of a change comes from a different model family than every maker of that change. A maker is any model that built or fixed the product's code under review, so a fixer's family joins the makers for every later round; a reviewer that writes break-it tests for a change is not a maker of it. When no reviewer from a different family than every maker can run, the review counts as not run, as for a planned reviewer that did not run, and no session picks a same-family reviewer or stands in for the reviewer on its own. Only the owner's word, for that one change, lets a reviewer from a maker's family run, and the review record then names that reviewer and says the review was not independent.

> **R6 — A single reviewer's reading, from the engine's own record.** On the light and micro lanes the planted-bug probe is gone (C2), and in its place the review record shows, from the engine's own record of the run, that the one reviewer read the change. A record that is missing, or that cannot tell whether the reviewer read the change, counts as not showing it; then the review counts as not run, the plugin runs the reviewer once more, and if the record still does not show a reading the change moves up to the full lane or parks. Which field of an engine's own record shows a reading, and how the choice between moving up and parking is made, are decide-by: C5.
