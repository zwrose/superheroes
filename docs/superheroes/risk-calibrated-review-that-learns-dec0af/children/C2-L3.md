What: Two ways of moving a review session retire, and the in-flight rule at upgrade is built. Today a review session can be moved to another worktree or replayed by relocate and re-emit (`cmd_re_emit` and the relocate verb in `plugins/superheroes/lib/round_driver.py`; `skills/review-code/reference/round-driver.md` § Moving a session — relocate and re-emit), and a review can start from an old saved session's records (`_seed_resume` in `round_driver.py`). This layer removes relocate, re-emit and starting from an old session's records (FR-5, fifth acceptance). Picking the same session back up after an interruption or damage stays exactly as it is; repairing a damaged session is C7's (UFR-5). A review in flight when a project takes the release finishes on the old version or restarts once on the new one, and the old certification files in its session folder stay on disk and are never read (UFR-10).
