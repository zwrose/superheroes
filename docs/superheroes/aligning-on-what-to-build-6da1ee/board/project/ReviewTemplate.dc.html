One review template, for any review

Tap sheets are a shared template, not a spec-review screen. Spec review uses it built in; any session may reach for it whenever there are calls for you to make. Every review gets the same card, so you only ever learn one.

The card, part by part · ruling 34
1Kind of call. A red badge for a gap or no source; plain for a question or a change to look at.
2The question, in one line, in your words where possible.
3Context: what's true now and why it needs you, plus the exact text it's about.
4Images, whenever the call has relevant ones: a board frame, a screenshot, a diagram. Tap to zoom; pinch on a phone.
5Options, when there are real ones, each with its plain consequence.
6Recommendation, one line, with the reason.
7Your answer: Aligned or Discuss, always; a direct pick when there are options; a note.
8The last card of a final sheet carries the verdict: Approve or Not yet.

No review gets its own buttons. A PR walk uses Aligned and Discuss too.

Who uses it · ruling 31
Built in, expected

Discovery's remainder sheets after the review rounds, and the final sheet after the advisor's vet.

Free to use, not expected

Any session may reach for it when it has calls for you: a PR walk, a merge review, a vet's questions. You can also ask for it. Nothing yet says these reviews must use it.

Where it runs

Phone first, desktop too. Answers save as you tap and go back to the session that sent the sheet.

Images, a standing rule: if a call has relevant images, the card includes them, every time, in every review.
