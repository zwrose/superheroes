The review cycle

Steps 9 to 13 of the flow, drawn as the process. Everything left of your tap sheet runs without you.

What each check asks
Gap review: clarity, testability, missing unhappy paths within the threat model, contradictions, safety and access.
Source check: forward, every statement has a source and matches it, board first, and nothing is another piece's; backward, every ruling in Canon for this piece, and the framing, is in the spec, written down right.
Grounding: every claim about the product or the repo, checked against the repo.
Who reviews
Each check is an independent reviewer from a different model family than the author, and the same reviewer continues across rounds.
Configure gains a spec reviewer seat, separate from code review: for example Astra for specs while Sol stays the deep code reviewer. The plugin names no model.
Declines: only five reasons
The finding is wrong, it's outside the threat model, it asks for implementation, it belongs to another spec, or it's already queued. Each one cites its proof, and every decline is listed, folded away, on your final sheet.
