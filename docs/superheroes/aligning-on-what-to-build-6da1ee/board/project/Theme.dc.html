Rules that keep it readable
  • Outlines are 3px ink; small parts 2px.
  • Offset shadows, never blurred: 6px on cards, 4px on small boxes, 3px on buttons.
  • Corners 6 to 14px. No gradients, no emoji.
  • Text meets 4.5 to 1 contrast; white only on blue, red or ink.
  • Colours that must be told apart also differ in lightness.
  • Touch targets at least 44px; images tap to zoom.
  • One theme for now. A dark variant can come later.
