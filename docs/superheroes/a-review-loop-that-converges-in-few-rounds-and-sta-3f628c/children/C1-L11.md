What: A PR that comes back for review is reviewed for what changed, on every lane. Today a new review session after a send-back, a fix leg, a catch-up with the base or a hand fix starts from round 1 with a full panel; the workhorse's post-handback table sends new surface or behaviour to the full loop again (`plugins/superheroes/skills/workhorse/SKILL.md` § 12); the light lane re-reviews to convergence on its one seat with no stated cap; a carried finding with no outcome can block the record for good; and a stack layer is not brought current while its review is open (`rubric/native-stacks.md` § How a stack stays current). This layer:
- has a new session review only what changed since the last reviewed commit, with the fix audit and scoped finder of a later round, and no full panel (FR-44);
- runs a full panel only when that change is over 1,000 countable lines (C1-L5's counter and constant) or touches a risk area the PR had not touched; a change whose risk is unclear counts as touching one, unless the PR's earlier review also treated its risk as unclear (FR-45);
- holds the light and micro lanes to the same 4-round cap and the same rule, so a light-lane record never shows a fifth round in one session except the one extra audit round (FR-52), in `skills/workhorse/SKILL.md` § 10 and § 12, `rubric/review-discipline.md` and the micro-lane doctrine;
- settles a finding carried from an abandoned session with no outcome as fixed when later commits show it fixed, and otherwise sends it back to the reviewers (UFR-1);
- has a session reviewing an upper stack layer catch up to the lower layer's current head before it reviews, and again when the lower layer moves during the session, so no finding comes from the lower layer's old head (UFR-2), amending the native-stacks rule above to say so. **Superseded by spec amendment #2 (2026-10-30):** the session keeps one fixed lower-layer commit and the layer catches up once at handback; C1-L11e builds it.
