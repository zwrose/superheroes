What: Everything the owner touches on a sheet, built on C7's shell and theme. Layer 1, cards and answers: every card except a final sheet's last card has the same parts in this order: the kind of call; the question in one line, in the owner's words where possible; the context (what's true now, why it needs the owner, the exact text the call is about); images when the call has relevant ones; options when there are real ones, each with its plain consequence; a one-line recommendation with its reason; the owner's answer; a note. Each offers Aligned and Discuss, plus a direct pick when it has options; Aligned agrees with the recommendation (or the statement when there is none), a pick chooses that option, Discuss leaves the call open for chat, and no review adds its own buttons. A card for a reviewer's gap, an unsourced statement or an unsettled finding carries a red warning badge; others carry a plain badge naming the kind of call. A tap saves at once as a draft the owner can change, the last tap counting (R18); a failed save says so on the card and offers a retry; the sheet is private to the owner by default, the owner may share it, and anyone else who opens it sees it but cannot answer. A usage doc says any session may use the template when it has calls for the owner, or when the owner asks, and names no expected use beyond discovery's sheets; that the sending session reads answers together once the owner says the sheet is done, never on a single tap, and treats a note that disagrees with its answer as Discuss; and the chat-prose fallback when the host can't show a sheet (R20). Layer 2, sheets: discovery's remainder sheet and final sheet are built-in sheet kinds; a sheet shows its review's name in a yellow app bar with answered-out-of-total; on a phone a remainder sheet folds answered items into one row with its Aligned and Discuss counts, shows the open item in full and lists the other open items as short rows with their state; on a desktop it lists every item with its state beside the open card, with Previous and Next; a remainder sheet says why it holds only these items (rounds run, fixes made, everything else tracing to the owner's board or rulings, apart from any finding marked not settled); "Done for now" leaves the sheet to come back to. Layer 3, the final sheet: it opens with how the spec got there (review rounds, fixes, the vet), lists the declined findings folded away, shows the vet's calls as rows with their answers, and ends with "Approve the spec?" saying every statement traces to the owner's board (where there is one), framing, rulings, answers or craft recorded for veto, and that the approved board is saved with it where there is one, offering Approve, Not yet and a note; below it, what happens next (R22). "Send verdict" sends the verdict with the sheet's answers in one action, a verdict tapped but not sent is a draft and never counts as approval, and once sent the sheet shows plainly beside the button that it was sent, the verdict and its note saved as a draft until sent and restored on reopen (R18). Layer 4, images: a card shows the images that bear on its call, in every review; tapping one opens it on its own, large, with Close returning to the card; pinch or double-tap zooms and drag looks around; no sideways swipe moves between images; an image that can't load leaves the question, context and answers in place and says the image is missing. **Register entries this child consumes (quoted verbatim from docs/superheroes/spec-alignment-landing-2026-10-05/register.md; the register-quote check runs at filing, intake, and the package read):**

> **R17 — The sheet data file.** A sheet is the plugin's review template published together with one data file that holds the sheet's cards; no session edits the template's code to make a sheet, and the data file is also the sheet's saved source. The data file names the sheet's kind (a remainder sheet, a final sheet, or a plain sheet for any other review) and its title; for a remainder sheet, the facts behind "why only these" (rounds run, fixes made, any finding the review didn't settle); for a final sheet, how the spec got there (review rounds, fixes, the vet), the folded declined findings, and the approval card's facts (whether there is an approved board and whether it is saved with the spec). Each card carries a stable id, the kind of call, whether it carries the warning badge, the question, its context (what is true now, why it needs the owner, the exact text), its images, its options each with a plain consequence, and a one-line recommendation with its reason. The data file's field names are decide-by: C7, which lands the schema with the template.

> **R18 — How answers come back.** A sheet is published private to the owner, and its own store holds the answers: each tap writes or replaces one document per card, keyed by the card's id, in an `answers` collection (the answer, which is Aligned, Discuss or an option, and the note), so the last tap counts; a final sheet's verdict (Approve or Not yet, with its note) is saved as a draft the moment it is tapped, in a draft-verdict document that never counts as approval, and becomes the sent verdict only when "Send verdict" writes the separate verdict document; reopening the sheet restores an unsent draft verdict. The session that sent the sheet reads every answer together once the owner says the sheet is done (on a final sheet, "Send verdict" is that word), never acts on a single tap, and treats a card whose note disagrees with its answer as Discuss. Another session can take a sheet over by reading its data file and its store; nothing assumes the owner has a second account.

> **R19 — The theme ships once.** The Comic panel theme ships as one stylesheet, with its fonts declared, at a path C7 names; the review template and every board's frame and labels load it, nothing else restates a theme colour, font or part rule, and a board draws the product itself in the project's design system, never in the theme. The path is decide-by: C7.

> **R20 — When a sheet can't be shown.** Where the host can't show a sheet, the session puts the same cards to the owner as numbered chat prose, each with its context, options and recommendation, built from the same data file; the template's usage doc states this once and discovery points at it.

> **R22 — After approval.** When the owner approves on the final sheet, the advisor adds the breakdown to the same spec PR (or beside the stored spec) and vets it; the existing independent package read still runs where it applies; one merge word covers both, and issues file with the owner's word as it merges. The final sheet's "what happens next" line says exactly this.

> **R24 — Glossary terms.** The glossary, `plugins/superheroes/rubric/glossary.md`, gains one entry each, under a stable heading, for Canon, ceded call and standing ruling (C1); the owner-vs-craft line (C2); remainder sheet and ready for vet (C4); and card and sheet (C8). Every other surface links the heading and states no definition of its own.
