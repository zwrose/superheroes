What: The plugin's one visual theme, Comic panel, shipped once as one stylesheet with its fonts declared, at a path this layer names (R19), for every sheet, every review page, and the frame and labels of every board, while a board draws the product itself in the project's design system. Each colour has one job: paper `#FFF8E7` for the ground; ink `#111111` for text, outlines, shadows and arrows; hero blue `#1B4FD8` for the owner's moments and the main action; alarm red `#D7263D` for warnings; highlight yellow `#FFC93C` for app bars, what's new and standing rulings; reviewer green `#0F7A4F` on `#E3F4EA` for independent reviewers only. Headings are set in Bricolage Grotesque 800, body text in Atkinson Hyperlegible 400 and 700, labels in the body font in tracked capitals, tags and Canon entries in IBM Plex Mono. Parts follow the drawn rules: 3px ink outlines (2px on small parts); offset shadows, never blurred (6px on cards, 4px on small boxes, 3px on buttons); corners of 6 to 14px; no gradients and no emoji. All text meets a 4.5 to 1 contrast ratio, white text sits only on blue, red or ink, every control is at least 44px tall, and colours that must be told apart also differ in lightness. A specimen page shows all of it. Pages published as claude.ai artifacts load fonts from Google Fonts only.
