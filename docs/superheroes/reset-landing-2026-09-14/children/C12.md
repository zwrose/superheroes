> **R1 — The vocabulary and its slugs.** The canonical glossary (one file, named by C1) carries one stable slug per term of Spec A FR-F9's seed, the whole seed and nothing less: ruling; decision walk; gardening pass, record, and window; condition window; keep-or-retire list; keep-or-retire entry; project configuration and configuration item; plugin component; retirement condition; catch-based, citation-based, and usage-based; real catch and noise; citation; background-session trial; consumer evidence; unmeasured; fired; proposal; retirement ruling; foundational component; authority surface; the priority tiers; declined registry, trigger, misses log, collector, and pending-words line; dial, machinery lane, product-forward lane, and N; the child names as reference vocabulary; craft call and owner call; material consequence; kind label; fold-in rules and folded-in items; workaround marker and delete-when condition; always-loaded set; keep list; rail; doctrine surface; foreign catch; the threat tags on the machinery map; and the four tags structural, capability-gap, harness-limit, and mixed; plus ground-truth sources (renamed 2026-09-13). Every other surface cites a slug; no surface restates a definition.

> **R5 — The receipt writer's boundary.** The certification receipt is derived by a receipt writer that is its own module: its only input is the review journal on disk, it imports nothing from the round driver, and its tests run with no driver. It emits the receipt or a refusal that names the class and the artifact. The receipt carries, beside today's fields: the terminal state, one of certified, cap, or cannot-certify; for a non-certified terminal its cause, either a loop reason from the closed set (latch, resume, state hash, detector, fixer cycle) or a non-loop reason (budget, open findings); each seat's provenance in its own field, dispatch-observed or hand-landed, kept separate from the certification shape; the disclosures block; and each finding's severity, disposition, and the receipt for that disposition.

> **R6 — Dispositions.** A finding has exactly one of three dispositions: fixed with a verification receipt on the certified head; refuted with a recorded reason; or out of scope with a named follow-up item that exists at certification time and carries a revisit trigger (a condition, date, or milestone, never "documented") and a class-closure line. Minor and Nit findings may take the third disposition by the review's orchestrator; an Important finding may take it only for a very good reason stated on the disposition, and the receipt writer lists every such Important finding in the receipt's disclosures block; a Critical finding may not take it at all, by anyone: fixed or refuted, or the review is uncertified and the PR merges only as owner-accepted in its body.

> **R7 — Engagement is telemetry.** Whenever the machine needs to know that a dispatched seat actually looked, the evidence is the runner's own record of the seat's tool calls and files opened, never a list the seat authored; for a dispatch-observed seat, absent or empty telemetry refuses. A hand-landed seat has no telemetry and is bound instead by FR-D2's execution-evidence binding on its landed envelope, labeled hand-landed on the receipt. The planted defect leaves every gate and becomes a sampled competence probe run after certification. The launcher's seat canary for builder sessions reads the session transcript's tool calls the same way.

> **R16 — The keep-or-retire entry.** Every plugin component has one entry on the keep-or-retire list, kept by a person in the keep-or-retire reference doc: the component and its cost or annoyance; its condition (catch-based, citation-based, or usage-based, with the window in calendar days, plugin defaults 45, 45, and 60) and the one action on firing, a proposal to the owner at a gardening pass; its last demonstrated benefit with a receipt or "unknown"; consumer evidence as receipts, "unmeasured" where none; the owner's latest decision; and notes carrying one of four tags and, for a component guarding a model behavior, the engine family. The foundational set at the stamp is the covenant's hard lines and nothing else; the authority surfaces (the worktree guard, the circuit breaker and escalation, model governance, the verify gate, the vet-receipt spine with its owner half) get the owner's one look at C6's landing. The workaround-marker inventory is a section of the same doc; a new marker's entry lands in the PR that adds the workaround.
