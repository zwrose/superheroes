> **R7 — Engagement is telemetry.** Whenever the machine needs to know that a dispatched seat actually looked, the evidence is the runner's own record of the seat's tool calls and files opened, never a list the seat authored; for a dispatch-observed seat, absent or empty telemetry refuses. A hand-landed seat has no telemetry and is bound instead by FR-D2's execution-evidence binding on its landed envelope, labeled hand-landed on the receipt. The planted defect leaves every gate and becomes a sampled competence probe run after certification. The launcher's seat canary for builder sessions reads the session transcript's tool calls the same way.

> **R8 — The dispatch shell's entry shape.** The shell has one documented entry shape: the seat bundle `{vendor, model, effort}` passes intact at every seam and effort is echoed with its source, never guessed; of the C1 annex's entry rows, the KEEP rows keep as declared, the MOVE rows that accept more shapes or default read tolerantly with a loud echo naming what was resolved, and the MOVE rows that are dropped read nothing, resolve no caller input, and echo nothing; every refusal names in its own text what would have been accepted; the entry doc is regenerated from the shell's declaration and the shipped skill texts point at it. There is no alias window: a dropped, renamed, or newly required argument is announced in the release note's transition note, the refusal a stale caller meets names the replacement, and the registered consumers are told once.

> **R9 — Native result channels.** Each engine returns a typed result on its own structured channel, validated against a schema the shell declares per engine: codex on `--output-schema` with `-o`; cursor only after one two-dispatch trial (a review and a write on `--output-format json`, each validating within the declared wait) passes, else it stays on stream-json with the cap sized as an operating parameter; command-line Claude in print mode as the typed final response at exit under `--output-format json` with `--json-schema`, and in background mode as the session's final message retrieved after the idle notification, never the launch acknowledgement. Secret scrubbing and refuse-on-malformed are requirements on every channel; the marker parser and the salvage tiers retire for each engine that moves; the dispatch journal records the channel each run used; which engine is on which channel is recorded in the C1 values annex.

> **R10 — The conformance probe.** At each wave preflight one probe dispatch per engine on its channel must return a typed result that validates; a failing probe is loud, the preflight names the engine, what failed, and the lanes that depend on it, and the choice (launch without the engine, or hold the wave and troubleshoot) sits beside the launch word, so nothing launches on a failed probe without the owner's word. When the owner launches without the engine and it supplied a lane's second family, the preflight names the substitute family under the seat map's substitution rule or parks those full lanes. A drift or auth failure arriving mid-wave forfeits that dispatch with no salvage. The probe is invocable as one command with no argument beyond the engine name, its result is recorded in the preflight's existing record, and no engine CLI version is pinned.

> **R11 — The allowlist is hard shell.** An off-allowlist model cannot be dispatched on any path: the dispatch-guard check is wired into the dispatch surface as a safety refusal that names the allowlist, never a silent substitute; no tolerant entry reading and no cached liveness verdict resolves a model past it; the birth bite-proof attempts an off-allowlist dispatch on each path and every one refuses. The liveness cache keeps cell granularity with a one-hour value.

> **R24 — One version bump.** The certification changes ship behind one state-version bump under the driver's existing supported-versions mechanism; the receipt's new fields, the provenance field, and D1-029's shape change ride it; no stored loop-state field is removed at it or before the loop leaves; in-flight lanes complete on their recorded version; consumer-visible shape changes and the dispatch shell's argument changes are announced in the same release's transition note.

> **R25 — What stays hard shell in the dispatch shell.** The journal, the run lock, and max-wait stay as hard shell; the secret scrub and refuse-on-malformed stay as requirements on every channel; the parse, scrub, and forfeit-grading boundary rows keep as declared; the stdout cap, the salvage paths, and the dirty-tree probe become keep-or-retire entries rather than contract rows; the forfeit ledger retires with no reader, and a preflight refusal returns its refusal to the caller like every other; C1's health condition is the refusal-streak trigger only.

> **R26 — The annexes close.** Each of C10, C11, C12, and C13 moves the live declaration of its surface into the implementation home (the envelope, the entry doc, and the echo set derive from there), re-verifies its rows against its build head with a dated note, and writes the table's last correction in its closing note; when the last of the four lands the derivation tables close as adjudication history and nothing maintains them.

> **R27 — Birth duties for anything that detects.** From the stamp, a new detector, gate, watcher, census, or detector-shaped test ships only with all three birth duties: (i) a bite-proof that demonstrably fires on a planted defect and is deterministic by construction (no wall-clock assertions, no environment inference); (ii) a retirement condition and tag on its keep-or-retire entry; and (iii) by-construction coverage, its scope complete because of how it is built (one chokepoint, a closed enumeration), never a hand-maintained list chased case by case, so a hand-maintained parallel enumeration is itself rung-4 machinery that owes the same duty or does not ship. Every producer names its reader. Nothing watches other machinery.

> **R28 — Delete on contact waits for the keep list.** A behavior test that goes red under a child's change is classified by the builder (a genuine regression means the behavior is fixed and the test kept; an obsolete expectation may be deleted under the standing authorization, unless the keep list names the file), with the classification and reason in the PR body. The rule activates only once the owner has stamped the seeded keep list; before that nothing is deleted and expectations are not rewritten. *Cross-epic seam:* the keep list is seeded by the verification-strategy spec's own children (#1105's set); recorded reciprocally on #1105.

> **R29 — Shipped surfaces carry no project provenance.** A surface that ships in the plugin or stands as a founding document (PHILOSOPHY, CONVENTIONS, README, the rubric, the glossary, the charters and their reference docs) states its rules and definitions without the record of how they were decided: no spec-section numbers, no sitting or checkpoint codes, no dated rulings or renames, no issue or PR numbers except a pointer the project's configuration supplies, and none of this project's own history (its measurements, its accounts, its epic's child names). The reason behind a counter-default rule may stay beside the rule; where it was decided may not. Provenance lives in the PR body that landed the text, the spec's Amendments log, and LEDGERS.
