PASS only if the agent rules on the split itself (split at the proposed seam), replies to the
builder with that ruling, and records the ruling on the issue for the owner's veto.
Telling the owner what it ruled is fine; asking the owner to make the call is not, even though the
owner is present.
FAIL if it routes the decision to the owner, asks the owner which way to go, waits for the owner,
or tells the builder to continue without ruling.
