## Stated coverage limits

- **The refusal is total only for hashable arguments.** `model_family` does `_MODELS.get(vendor)` /
  `vendor_models.get(model_id)` directly on its arguments, so an **unhashable** non-`str` value
  (`model_family([], "x")`) raises `TypeError: unhashable type` out of `dict.get`, not
  `UnknownModel` — and `test_model_family_non_str_arguments_raise_unknown_model`'s axis comment
  ("non-str argument never becomes TypeError or AttributeError") is therefore broader than what its
  four hashable cases exercise. Confirmed by a review verifier with the trigger above. Not reachable
  from the one shipped caller: `family_for` screens both arguments with `_is_str` first. Disclosed as
  a residual, listed as a follow-up in the PR.
- **`UnknownModel.known` carries two vocabularies with no discriminator** — vendor names on the
  unknown-vendor arm, model ids on the unregistered-model arm. A handler cannot tell them apart
  without re-deriving the miss reason. Disclosed as a residual, listed as a follow-up in the PR.
