## Residual

This proof does not claim the writer detects every fabrication. A forger who makes the blob
internally consistent — recomputing the digest over the bytes they planted — passes step 8, and a
forger who also writes a matching `fixContentDigest` onto the disposition receipt passes step 9. A
journal-only writer that may not read git cannot verify that a read ever happened. Splitting the two
digests across two separately-written artifacts raises the cost of a forgery; it does not close it.
