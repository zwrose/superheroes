## G2 — `_escape_guidance_placeholder_syntax`

**Guarded element:** `round_driver.py` `_escape_guidance_placeholder_syntax`. Axis: owner guidance
can neither inject an order placeholder nor wedge the render. `render_order` substitutes by
iterating a **set** of template placeholders with sequential `str.replace`, then refuses on any
`{{…}}` left behind — so unescaped owner text is both an injection vector and a denial-of-render.

**Neutralization:** returned the text unescaped:
```
-    return text.replace("{{", "{ {")
```

## What the probes established, stated before the probes themselves

The invariant under guard is **the string `_gate_guidance_block` returns contains no `{{`, whatever
any row field holds**. WO-C ships *three* mechanisms toward it, and probing them one at a time showed
**two of the three are dominated** — individually non-biting, because a survivor is caught downstream:

| Mechanism | Neutralized alone | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-point loop in `_escape_guidance_placeholder_syntax` | tests stay **green** | dominated by the exit post-condition |
| Per-field escaping of the header id/title | tests stay **green** | dominated by the exit post-condition |
| Exit post-condition in `_gate_guidance_block` | tests stay **green** | dominated by the other two |

That is a real result, not a formality: **no single-element probe on this surface can bite**, because
the three mechanisms are mutually redundant by design — the post-condition exists precisely to hold
the invariant for fields nobody has added yet. Reporting a single-element red here would have required
choosing a mutation that misrepresents the design. The proofs below therefore neutralize the
**minimal set that actually breaks the invariant**, and the dominance is disclosed rather than hidden
behind a green run. **A single-element G7 and G8 as originally ordered would have been vacuous** —
that is the finding, and it is recorded rather than papered over.
