### The structural error this round found and fixed

The previous round proved a fixture test by **deleting the census check's `hits.append(...)`** so the
fixture's `pytest.raises` would report `DID NOT RAISE`. That is a detector edited to make itself go
red — vacuous by the bite-proof rubric. Worse, in BP-3's case the recorded edit was *syntactically
impossible*: `hits.append(...)` is the sole statement in its `if` branch, so deleting it is an
`IndentationError` at import, and the recorded `DID NOT RAISE` traceback could not have come from it.

The correct proof of a census check is to **introduce the violation it exists to catch into real
skill content** and watch the shipped check go red. Those are the proofs recorded below.
