### Before you upgrade

- **After switching.** **A caller that shows why a decision a finding cites is not on record reads the finding's new optional `decisionNotOnRecord` key; while any lane still runs an earlier version, it reads both that key and any such warning in `reason` as a pointer to check the cited decision, not as its current state.** The writer no longer adds that warning to the finding's `reason`. See [Review record: the not-on-record warning moves to its own field](#review-record-the-not-on-record-warning-moves-to-its-own-field).

### Review record: the not-on-record warning moves to its own field

- **The change.** When the record writer finds that a decision a finding cites is not on record, the finding stays or becomes `left-for-owner`, keeps its reason as it was, and carries the failure in a new optional key, `findings[].decisionNotOnRecord`, a non-blank string. Before, the warning that the cited decision, ruling or bound is not on record was added to the finding's reason, and a ruling that later resolved kept those stale words. The writer now adds no such words, and drops the key from any finding whose outcome is no longer `left-for-owner`.
- **How to read it.** A record does not say which version wrote it (every record reads `review-record/1`), and while any lane still runs an earlier version, one PR's records can hold findings both versions wrote: an earlier writer keeps old warning words in `reason`, and can carry the key forward onto a finding whose ruling has since resolved. So until every lane that writes a PR's records runs this version, read the key and any old warning words in `reason` as a pointer to check the cited decision on the PR, never as its current state. Once every such lane runs this version, the key is the not-on-record failure as of the record's write, and warning words in `reason` are history.
- **Who reads it.** The PR sheet shows the key's text after the reason only for a finding listed for the merge verdict; the author of an owner card reads the key when writing the card's context. The account validator refuses the key when it is not a non-blank string.
- **Earlier records.** A finding stamped by an earlier version keeps its words in `reason`, including when a later session carries it into a new record, whatever its outcome there (a resolved `ruling`, `fixed`, or still `left-for-owner`); nothing rewrites them.
- Nothing is removed or renamed.
