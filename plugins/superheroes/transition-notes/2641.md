### Before you upgrade

- **After switching.** **A caller that shows why a decision a finding cites is not on record reads the finding's new optional `decisionNotOnRecord` key, and keeps reading `reason` for records written before this version.** The writer no longer adds that warning to the finding's `reason`. See [Review record: the not-on-record warning moves to its own field](#review-record-the-not-on-record-warning-moves-to-its-own-field).

### Review record: the not-on-record warning moves to its own field

- **The change.** When the record writer finds that a decision a finding cites is not on record, the finding stays or becomes `left-for-owner`, keeps its reason as it was, and carries the failure in a new optional key, `findings[].decisionNotOnRecord`, a non-blank string. Before, the warning that the cited decision, ruling or bound is not on record was added to the finding's reason, and a ruling that later resolved kept those stale words. A ruling stamped by this version that later resolves reads clean, and the writer drops the key from any finding whose outcome is no longer `left-for-owner`.
- **Who reads it.** The PR sheet shows the key's text after the reason only for a finding listed for the merge verdict; an owner card shows only the context its author writes, so read `decisionNotOnRecord` when you write that card. A caller that looked for the warning inside `reason` reads the key when it is present, and still reads `reason` for an earlier record, since reading a stored record adds no key. The account validator refuses the key when it is not a non-blank string.
- **Earlier records.** A finding stamped by an earlier version keeps its words in `reason`, and so does that finding when a later session carries it into a new record, even once its ruling resolves; nothing rewrites them. Read such words in a `ruling` finding's reason as history: `decisionNotOnRecord` is the only current failure.
- Nothing is removed or renamed.
