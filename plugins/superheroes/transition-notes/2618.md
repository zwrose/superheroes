### Before you upgrade

- **After switching.** **Nothing is owed by a caller: a second-test or test-correction seat whose answer names none of its items is now asked once more, once per round for each of the two logical phases, before its findings fold.** No argument is dropped, renamed or newly required. See [Second test: a silent seat is asked once more](#second-test-a-silent-seat-is-asked-once-more).

### Second test: a silent seat is asked once more

- **The change.** When a second-test seat answers none of the items its order gave, the review driver no longer folds that answer at once. Once per round for each logical phase (`second-test`, and `second-test-correction` for a test-correction order), it sends the same order again with one added line naming the ids not answered. A second silence folds exactly as an empty answer did before, so every review still reaches its end. The verifiers are not asked again.
- **What a reader sees differently.** The re-sent order's payload gains `askedAgain`, the list of ids the earlier answer left out. The decisions gain two kinds, `second-test-asked-again` and `break-it-correction-asked-again`. The round record gains `askedAgain`, the list of logical phases whose one re-ask is spent; it is carried in `state.json`, so a resumed session never asks again. A `silentSeats` row recorded after the re-ask carries `askedAgain: true`, and its decision and its `silent-seat` degradation line end "after the seat was asked once more" where they ended "and nothing was re-asked".
- Replacement. None: the change is additive, and no key, token, argument or result shape is renamed or removed.
