### Before you upgrade

- **After switching.** **Nothing is owed by a caller that does not read the new keys: a verifier, second-test or test-correction answer that names none of the items it was given is recorded as silent.** The round gains `silentSeats`, the decisions gain `verifiers-silent`, `second-test-silent` or `break-it-correction-silent`, and the review's degradations gain a `silent-seat` line; the items fold as before. A verifier is not asked again; a second-test or test-correction seat is first asked once more (see "Second test: a silent seat is asked once more"). See [Review loop: a seat that answers none of its items is recorded as silent](#review-loop-a-seat-that-answers-none-of-its-items-is-recorded-as-silent).

### Review loop: a seat that answers none of its items is recorded as silent

- **The rule.** An answer that names none of the ids its order gave is silent. The round's `silentSeats` gains a row: `{"phase", "grain": "answer", "items": <count>}`, or, for verifier verdicts assembled from each cluster's own seat, `{"phase": "verifiers", "grain": "seat", "items": <count>, "seats": [...]}`. One decision is recorded (`verifiers-silent`, `second-test-silent` or `break-it-correction-silent`), and the degradations gain "silent-seat (round <n>): <who> answered none of its <n> item(s); they are recorded as unanswered (unverified or no new test) and nothing was re-asked" (for a second-test or test-correction seat the line ends "after the seat was asked once more" instead; see "Second test: a silent seat is asked once more"). A seat that never ran is not judged silent; it stays in `unverifiedClusters`.
- **The verifier assembly.** The assembled verifier artifact gains `verifierSilentClusters` (seat names) and `verifierSilentItems`, and the disclosures gain `silentClusters`; a submitted artifact's own copies of the two keys are dropped before the fold.
- **The second-test order.** Its delivery text now asks the seat to return every finding it was given exactly once, with its staged id, and never an empty findings list.
- **What did not change.** An unanswered verifier item stays unverified, a held finding goes to the second pass with its first test, and a verifier is not asked again. A second-test or test-correction seat is asked once more before its items fold (see "Second test: a silent seat is asked once more").
- Replacement. None: every key, kind and line is additive.
