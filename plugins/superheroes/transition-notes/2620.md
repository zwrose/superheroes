### Before you upgrade

- **After switching.** **Nothing is owed by a caller: a build that starts at a head another lane produced re-runs every bite-proof record its branch carries and posts the tally in its build record.** No command, argument or result changes. See [Recovery: inherited bite-proofs are re-run on the new head](#recovery-inherited-bite-proofs-are-re-run-on-the-new-head).

### Recovery: inherited bite-proofs are re-run on the new head

- **The change.** A build whose worktree starts at a head another lane produced (a resumption after a death, an adoption, or a stack layer built on a sibling's head) re-runs every bite-proof record the branch carries on its own head before new work, posts the tally (reproduced / could-not-bite / stale receipt) in the build record, and fixes in-lane whatever no longer bites. `rubric/launch-doctrine.md` § Recovery and the workhorse charter's intake carry the item in the same words.
- **Where the tally goes.** The build record carries the inherited bite-proof tally directly after the bite-proof records, one entry per inherited record with its outcome and, for each that no longer bites, the in-lane fix; a build that inherited no record writes `Inherited bite-proofs: none`.
- Replacement. None: no key, token, argument or result shape changes.
