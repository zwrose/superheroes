Rules that keep it readable

  • Outlines are ink, small parts .
  • Offset shadows, never blurred: on cards, on boxes, on buttons.
  • Corners run to , no gradients, no emoji.
  • Text meets 4.5 to 1 contrast, white only on blue, red or ink.
  • Colours that must be told apart also differ in lightness.
  • Every control is at least tall.
  • One theme for now.
